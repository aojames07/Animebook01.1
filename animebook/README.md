# 🎌 Animebook // Personal Anime Logbook

A sleek, modern web application built directly for your custom **Anime Card Template**.

## 🚀 Quick Start

1. Open `index.html` in any web browser (Chrome, Edge, Firefox, Brave, etc.) by double-clicking it.
2. No server, Node.js, or internet connection required! Everything runs locally and offline.

---

## ✨ Features

- **Exact Template Fields**:
  - **Title**: Custom title with inline editing
  - **Format**: `TV`, `Movie`, `OVA`, `ONA`, `Special`
  - **Studio**: Custom studio name
  - **Genre**: Multiple genre tags
  - **Status**: `Plan to Watch`, `Watching`, `Completed`, `On Hold`, `Dropped`
  - **Progress**: Episode tracker with quick `+` and `-` buttons plus a live visual progress bar
  - **Score**: Star rating out of 10
  - **Favorite Character**: Track your favorite character for each show
  - **Watch Notes & Live Thoughts**:
    - 🎬 **Ep 1-5**: First impressions & opening hooks
    - ⚡ **Mid-Season Arc**: Major twists, climax & character development
    - 🏁 **Final Thoughts**: Overall verdict, review & themes
  - **Favorite Quote / Highlight**: Stylized quote banner for memorable dialogue

- **1-Click Template Export**:
  - Click **📋** on any card to copy its exact text formatted according to your template directly into your clipboard.
  - Click **Copy Blank Template** in the header to get an empty template ready to paste anywhere.

- **Auto-Save & Offline Storage**:
  - All changes are automatically persisted to your browser's `localStorage` in real time with an auto-save indicator.

- **Search & Filter**:
  - Fast search by title, studio, genre, character, notes, or quote.
  - Status tabs (`All`, `Watching`, `Plan to Watch`, `Completed`, `On Hold`, `Dropped`).
  - Sort by recently added, title (A-Z), score, or progress percentage.

- **Backup & Portability**:
  - **Export MD**: Export your entire logbook as a clean Markdown file.
  - **Backup (JSON)**: Save a JSON snapshot of your data.
  - **Restore (JSON)**: Restore or merge entries on any device.
